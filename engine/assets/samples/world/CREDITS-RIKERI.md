# VRChat two-storey training building

Rikard Eriksen (rikeri) modeled the two-storey interior as a teaching example for [Making your own room for VRChat](https://github.com/madjin/vrchat-room-tutorial). [Original model commit](https://github.com/madjin/vrchat-room-tutorial/commit/a0d26a8fd6c8000bb66a482e7df669a8a949dba7) · [Blender source](https://github.com/madjin/vrchat-room-tutorial/blob/870b99834208ec928d16a83079020037624f3276/multiroom.blend).

The WebGL adaptation exports the single-piece example from the source file and centers its base. It preserves the original mesh, three material groups and floor plan. New PBR colors distinguish the walls, timber floor and glazing. The downloadable GLB also embeds the license text.
